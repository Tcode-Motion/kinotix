❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Find instant answers to over 40 technical and usage questions.

1. What is KinotiX? +
KinotiX is a flagship open-source Android wallpaper app featuring 3D Gyroscope Parallax, OpenGL ES Fluid dynamics, Live Camera backgrounds, and 4K Ultra HD visual art.
2. Is KinotiX free to use? +
Yes! KinotiX is 100% free with optional rewarded ads to unlock 4K master downloads.
3. How does the 3D Gyro Engine work? +
It reads hardware rotation sensors via Android SensorManager to tilt background artwork layers in real-time.
4. How does the OpenGL ES Fluid Engine work? +
It executes native C++/OpenGL ES shaders to simulate fluid velocity and density equations when you touch your screen.
