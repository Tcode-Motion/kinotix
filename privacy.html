Privacy Policy & Transparency Notice

Effective Date: July 30, 2026 | Application: KinotiX | Package Name: com.kinotix.app

1. Introduction & Core Privacy Philosophy

KinotiX ("we", "our", or "us") respects your personal privacy. We do not require user account registration, social login, names, email addresses, phone numbers, or physical location addresses. This Privacy Policy provides full transparency regarding the Android permissions and third-party SDKs integrated into KinotiX (Package Name: com.kinotix.app) in strict compliance with the Google Play Developer Policy and Data Safety disclosures.

2. Android System Permissions & Purpose

KinotiX requests only the Android system permissions necessary to deliver core wallpaper features:

Permission NameTypePurpose & Strict Data Handling
android.permission.INTERNET
android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE		 Network Streams high-resolution wallpaper catalog items, WebP thumbnails, and checks network connection (Wi-Fi vs mobile data).
android.permission.SET_WALLPAPER
android.permission.SET_WALLPAPER_HINTS		 System Wallpaper Applies static, 3D Parallax, OpenGL ES Fluid, and Video wallpapers directly to your Home and Lock screens.
android.permission.CAMERA
android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE_CAMERA		 Hardware Camera Used strictly to stream real-time camera preview background for the Transparent Camera live wallpaper.
Notice: No camera video or photos are ever recorded, saved, uploaded, or transmitted anywhere.
android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS Notifications Optional Android 13+ permission used to deliver push notifications regarding fresh wallpaper drops and collection updates.
android.permission.WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE Storage (legacy) Used on Android 9 (API 28) and below to save downloaded wallpapers to gallery. Android 10+ uses standard MediaStore API.
android.permission.WAKE_LOCK System Power Holds temporary wake locks while initializing hardware sensors and GPU wallpaper surface setup.

3. Integrated Third-Party SDKs & Services

A. Google AdMob (Advertising)

KinotiX integrates Google AdMob (App ID: ca-app-pub-6497553076878553~7081144164) to serve Banner, Native, Interstitial, and Rewarded Ads (used to unlock Ultra HD 4K master downloads). AdMob may collect non-personally identifiable Advertising IDs (AAID), coarse IP location, and ad interaction metrics to serve relevant ads. Learn more at Google Privacy & Terms.

B. Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM Push Notifications)

Used to route opt-in push notifications for new wallpaper releases. FCM processes anonymous device registration tokens to route notification payloads.

C. Firebase Crashlytics & Analytics

By default, Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics collection is disabled (firebase_analytics_collection_enabled = false). When explicitly opted in by the user, Crashlytics collects anonymous crash stack traces, device model, and OS version to fix bugs and improve performance.

4. Google Play Data Safety Compliance Summary

  • Data Collected: Non-personal Advertising ID (AdMob), Device Crash Logs (Opt-in Crashlytics), Notification Tokens (FCM).
  • Data Shared: Non-personal Advertising ID shared with Google AdMob for ad rendering.
  • On-Device Local Storage: User category preferences, search history, favorites, and offline wallpaper caches are saved 100% locally in SQLite Room DB and SharedPreferences.
  • Data Security: All network requests use HTTPS (TLS 1.2/1.3) encryption. Cleartext HTTP traffic is disabled.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Families Policy)

KinotiX complies with COPPA and Google Play Families policy requirements. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

6. Data Control & Deletion Rights

Because KinotiX does not store user data on remote servers or require user accounts, uninstalling KinotiX immediately and permanently purges all local databases, preferences, and cached files from your device.

7. Contact Support

If you have any questions or data safety inquiries regarding KinotiX, please contact us via our official issue tracker at GitHub Issue Support.
