Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 30, 2026 | Last Updated: July 30, 2026 | Application: KinotiX (com.kinotix.app)

1. Introduction

KinotiX ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to maintaining user privacy and transparency. This Privacy Policy outlines how information is handled when you use the KinotiX Android mobile application (Package Name: com.kinotix.app).

2. Information We Do Not Collect

KinotiX is designed to operate without requiring unnecessary personal identifier collection. KinotiX does not collect or require:

  • User accounts or personal profile registration
  • Personal names or passwords
  • Email addresses or phone numbers
  • Personal contacts list
  • SMS or call log data
  • Calendar events or personal document files
  • Precise GPS location data

3. Android System Permissions & Purpose

KinotiX requests only the system permissions necessary to provide wallpaper features:

PermissionTypeFunctional Purpose
INTERNET
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE		 Network Access Streams online wallpaper catalog items and checks connectivity state (e.g., Wi-Fi vs. cellular network).
SET_WALLPAPER
SET_WALLPAPER_HINTS		 System Wallpaper Applies static, 3D Parallax, OpenGL ES Fluid, and Video wallpapers to your Home and Lock screens.
CAMERA
FOREGROUND_SERVICE_CAMERA		 Hardware Camera Camera data is processed locally on your device to provide the Transparent Camera wallpaper feature and is not uploaded to remote servers.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Notifications Optional Android 13+ permission used to deliver push notifications regarding wallpaper drops and updates.
WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE Storage Access Used on Android 9 and lower to save wallpapers to your media gallery. Android 10 and higher uses standard MediaStore API access.
WAKE_LOCK System Power Used temporarily during active rendering setup for dynamic live wallpaper surfaces.

4. Third-Party Services & Advertising

KinotiX utilizes third-party SDK services to support monetization, notifications, and application stability:

  • Google AdMob: KinotiX uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may process non-personal advertising identifiers and device data in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): Used to route opt-in push notifications regarding new wallpaper drops.
  • Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics: Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics collection is disabled by default in application configuration. When enabled, non-personal diagnostic logs help diagnose application crashes.

5. Local Device Storage

Information such as favorites, onboarding category preferences, search history, and cached wallpapers is stored locally on your device to provide offline wallpaper capability.

6. Data Retention & Uninstall Behavior

Most locally stored data is removed when the application is uninstalled, although Android system behavior may vary depending on device manufacturer and system backup settings.

7. Children's Privacy

KinotiX complies with COPPA and Google Play Families policy guidelines. KinotiX does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

8. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page, and the Effective Date will be updated accordingly.

9. Contact Information

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, you may contact us through:
