❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Find quick answers to over 40 common questions about KinotiX.

1. What is KinotiX? +
KinotiX is a flagship Android wallpaper app featuring 3D Gyroscope Parallax, OpenGL ES Fluid dynamics, Live Camera backgrounds, and 4K Ultra HD visual art.
2. Is KinotiX free to use? +
Yes! KinotiX is 100% free with optional rewarded ads to unlock 4K master downloads.
3. Which Android versions are supported? +
KinotiX supports Android 7.0 (API 24) and higher, up to Android 15 (API 35).
4. How does the 3D Gyroscope Engine work? +
It reads hardware rotation sensors via Android SensorManager to tilt background artwork layers in real-time, creating a realistic depth illusion.
5. How does the OpenGL ES Fluid Engine work? +
It executes native C++/OpenGL ES shaders to simulate fluid velocity and density equations in real time when you touch your screen.
6. Does Camera Live Wallpaper record video? +
No! Camera permission is strictly used to stream the live preview onto your screen background. Zero video is recorded or transmitted.
7. Where are downloaded wallpapers stored? +
Wallpapers are saved to your device's public Pictures/KinotiX folder via the MediaStore API.
8. Are Firebase Analytics enabled by default? +
No. In compliance with GDPR and CCPA, Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics are disabled by default until you explicitly opt in.
