🔒 100% Local & Offline Execution

The camera feed is processed strictly on your local device memory in real-time. No internet connection is used or required for camera functionality.

🌐 Zero Server Transmission & Sharing

No image, camera frames, video data, metadata, or telemetry are ever uploaded to any external server or shared with third parties.

📹 No Video Recording — Live Preview Only

The app performs zero video or photo recording and saves no media to disk. It only renders a real-time live preview stream directly onto the wallpaper surface.

⚡ Scoped Strictly to Live Camera Wallpaper

Camera permission and FOREGROUND_SERVICE_CAMERA are active ONLY while using the Transparent Camera Live Wallpaper. If you switch to another wallpaper (e.g. 3D Gyro or Fluid), the camera service completely turns off.
