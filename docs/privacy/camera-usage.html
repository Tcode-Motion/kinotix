Foreground Camera Service & Privacy Audit

Detailed technical disclosure for the Android android.permission.CAMERA and FOREGROUND_SERVICE_CAMERA usage in KinotiX (com.kinotix.app).

📱 Real-Device Demonstration Video

Foreground Camera Service Screen Recording Demo

This screen recording demonstrates how the camera permission is requested and executed strictly for real-time live wallpaper preview.

🎥 Live Phone Video Capture — Android Foreground Service Active

🔒 100% Offline Processing

Camera buffers are processed locally in device volatile memory (RAM). Absolutely zero network communication or cloud streaming occurs.

🌐 Zero Data Transmission

No photos, video streams, or image metadata are ever saved, stored, or transmitted to any third party or remote server.

📹 Preview Only (No Recording)

The app performs zero audio/video recording. It provides only a live preview feed projected directly onto the home and lock screen wallpaper.

⚡ Strictly Scoped Lifecycle

Camera hardware access and the foreground service terminate immediately when switching away from the Transparent Camera Live Wallpaper.
