🔒 Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 30, 2026 | Last Updated: July 30, 2026

KinotiX ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how your information is handled when you use the KinotiX Android mobile application (Package Name: com.kinotix.app).

1. Information We Collect

KinotiX is designed with an opt-in, privacy-first stance. We do not require account registration, and we do not collect your real name, email address, phone number, physical address, or contact list.

A. Local On-Device Data

  • User Preferences & Search Keywords: Your selected category onboarding preferences and search terms are saved strictly locally on your device in SharedPreferences.
  • Favorites & Offline Caching: Wallpapers favorited or saved for offline access are stored in a local SQLite database (Room DB).

B. Device Permissions & System Features

  • android.permission.INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used solely to fetch public high-resolution wallpaper catalog items and online feeds.
  • android.permission.SET_WALLPAPER & SET_WALLPAPER_HINTS: Required to set static and dynamic live wallpapers on your device home/lock screen.
  • android.permission.CAMERA & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_CAMERA: Required strictly to render the live camera stream for the Live Camera Transparent Wallpaper feature. No camera feed is ever recorded, stored, uploaded, or transmitted anywhere.
  • android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Required on Android 13+ to send optional push notifications regarding new wallpaper collections. You may revoke this permission at any time.
  • android.permission.WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE: Used on Android 9 (API 28) and below to save wallpapers to gallery. Android 10+ uses standard MediaStore API without broad storage access.

2. Third-Party Services & SDKs

KinotiX integrates trusted third-party SDKs to deliver core functionality and support development:

  • Google AdMob: Used to serve advertisements and rewarded ads (to unlock Ultra HD 4K downloads). AdMob may collect non-personally identifiable advertising IDs according to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): Used to deliver opt-in push notifications.
  • Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics: Disabled by default (firebase_analytics_collection_enabled = false). Users may opt in via Settings to share anonymous crash diagnostics.

3. Children's Privacy

KinotiX complies with COPPA and Google Play Designed for Families guidelines. We do not knowingly collect any personal data from children under the age of 13.

4. Data Retention & User Rights

Because all user preference data, favorites, and offline caches reside locally on your device, you have complete control over your data. Uninstalling KinotiX immediately and permanently removes all stored app data from your device.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us via email at kinotix.app@gmail.com.
