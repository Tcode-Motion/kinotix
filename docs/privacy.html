🔒 Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 30, 2026 | Last Updated: July 30, 2026

KinotiX ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy governs the KinotiX Android mobile application (Package Name: com.kinotix.app).

1. Information We Collect & Device Permissions

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Fetch online high-resolution wallpaper catalog items.
  • SET_WALLPAPER & SET_WALLPAPER_HINTS: Set wallpapers on device home and lock screens.
  • CAMERA & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_CAMERA: Strictly used for rendering the live transparent camera wallpaper preview. Zero camera feeds are recorded, saved, or transmitted.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Optional push notifications on Android 13+.
  • WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE: Used on Android 9 and below to save wallpapers to gallery. Android 10+ uses MediaStore API.

2. Third-Party SDKs

  • Google AdMob: Serves advertisements and rewarded ads (to unlock Ultra HD 4K downloads).
  • Firebase Messaging: Delivers optional push notifications.
  • Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics: Disabled by default (firebase_analytics_collection_enabled = false).
