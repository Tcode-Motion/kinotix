🔋 Keep Live Wallpapers Running Smoothly

Some Android manufacturers forcefully terminate background wallpaper services to conserve battery power. Follow the brand-specific steps below to disable aggressive task killing for KinotiX:

Samsung (One UI) Instructions

  1. Open device Settings > Apps > KinotiX.
  2. Tap Battery and select Unrestricted.
  3. Go back to Settings > Battery and device care > Battery.
  4. Tap Background usage limits > ensure KinotiX is added to Never sleeping apps.

Xiaomi / Redmi (MIUI / HyperOS) Instructions

  1. Open device Settings > Apps > Manage apps > KinotiX.
  2. Enable Autostart toggle.
  3. Tap Battery Saver and select No restrictions.

OnePlus / Oppo / Realme (OxygenOS / ColorOS) Instructions

  1. Open Settings > Apps > App management > KinotiX.
  2. Tap Battery usage > enable Allow background activity and Allow auto-launch.

Vivo / iQOO (Funtouch OS) Instructions

  1. Open Settings > Battery > High background power consumption.
  2. Enable the toggle for KinotiX.

Google Pixel / Stock Android Instructions

  1. Open Settings > Apps > See all apps > KinotiX.
  2. Tap App battery usage and select Unrestricted.

🛠️ Comprehensive Troubleshooting Guides

1. Live Wallpaper Stops Moving or Freezes

Symptoms: Fluid physics or 3D Gyro tilt stops animating after unlocking phone.

Possible Cause: OEM battery optimization force-closed background services.

Step-by-Step Solution: Follow the Battery Optimization guide above to mark KinotiX as Unrestricted. Re-open KinotiX and apply the wallpaper again.

2. Live Camera Wallpaper Shows Black Screen

Symptoms: Transparent Camera wallpaper screen is completely black.

Possible Cause: Camera permission not granted or background camera service blocked.

Step-by-Step Solution: Go to Android Settings > Apps > KinotiX > Permissions > Camera > select Allow only while using app. Re-select Live Camera wallpaper.

3. Wallpaper Download Failed

Symptoms: Tapping download button shows 'Download failed' toast error.

Possible Cause: No internet connection or mobile data restrict turned on.

Step-by-Step Solution: Verify your internet connection. If 'Wi-Fi Only Downloads' is turned on in KinotiX Settings, connect to a Wi-Fi network before downloading.

4. 3D Gyroscope Motion Not Responding

Symptoms: Wallpaper does not tilt when phone is rotated.

Possible Cause: Device lacks hardware rotation vector sensor or sensor permissions.

Step-by-Step Solution: Verify your device contains a hardware gyroscope. On devices without gyroscope hardware, KinotiX smoothly falls back to touch drag motion.
